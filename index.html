A Promise Kept in Time

Can a love once buried be raised? "A Promise Kept in Time" is a story told in whispers and shadows, a visual poem pieced together from fleeting moments against a twilight sky. It is a journey into a love torn apart by a choice cloaked as duty, and the silent, aching aftermath that forces a soul to build its own armor. This story explores the irreversible changes we endure and the stillness that profound loss teaches. When a vow of return finally burns through years of silence, it doesn't bring easy answers. Instead, it leaves one lingering, haunting question: can what was broken ever truly be made whole again?
